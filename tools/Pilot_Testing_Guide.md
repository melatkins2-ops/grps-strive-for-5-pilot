# GRPS Strive for Less Than 5 — Pilot Testing Guide

## Purpose
Test whether staff can use the resource to make an attendance decision quickly and correctly. This is not a feature brainstorming session.

## Pilot Roles
Include at least one user from each applicable role:
- Principal / School Leader
- Teacher
- Family Support Specialist
- KSSN Coordinator (partner school)
- Secretary / Attendance Staff
- Social Worker / Counselor
- District/Department Staff
- Other

## Three Required Tasks
### Task 1 — Support a Scholar
Give the tester a short attendance scenario. Ask the tester to:
1. Identify the attendance level.
2. Identify the likely root cause.
3. Find a matched intervention.
4. Identify the owner and evidence to monitor.

**Success:** correct next step in no more than 3 clicks from the homepage.

### Task 2 — Find a Tool
Ask the tester to locate one specific tool such as the Attendance Plan Template, Caring Conversation Guide, or 10/20/30-Day Review.

**Success:** tool located in no more than 3 clicks.

### Task 3 — Work From My Role
Ask the tester to choose their role and identify the most relevant starting resource.

**Success:** tester understands what their role owns and what to do next.

## Observe; Do Not Coach
Record:
- clicks
- hesitation
- wrong turns
- terminology the tester does not understand
- links/buttons they expect to be clickable
- whether the user can explain Action vs. Intervention after using the site

## Feedback Rule
Capture a problem before proposing a solution. Example:
- Good: “Three users could not find the review template.”
- Avoid: “Add another homepage card.”

## Release Thresholds
Before production release:
- No critical broken navigation or downloads
- 90%+ completion of the three required tasks
- Median 3 clicks or fewer for required tasks
- No recurring Action vs. Intervention misunderstanding
- No recurring role-ownership confusion
- All factual/content corrections resolved
- Mobile/keyboard/accessibility checks completed
