# Pilot Release Decision

## GO
Release when:
- no critical defects remain;
- required-task completion is at least 90%;
- median path is 3 clicks or fewer;
- staff distinguish an action from an intervention;
- role ownership is consistently understood;
- content corrections are closed; and
- accessibility/mobile checks are complete.

## GO WITH MINOR FIXES
Use only when remaining issues do not affect accuracy, decision-making, navigation, accessibility, or required downloads.

## HOLD
Hold release for:
- broken primary pathways;
- inaccurate attendance guidance;
- intervention/referral confusion;
- recurring role confusion;
- inaccessible core functionality;
- missing required tool/download;
- recurring inability to complete a required task.
