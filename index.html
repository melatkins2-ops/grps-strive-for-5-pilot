Action or intervention?

An action is something staff did, like a call, letter or referral. An intervention is designed to change the reason for absence, and it is monitored.

Root causes of absence
Is the intervention working? (10/20/30-day decisions)

At each checkpoint for an active Tier 2 or Tier 3 plan, review three things: Was the intervention delivered? Did the barrier improve? Is attendance improving?

Attendance percentage informs the level of support. The identified root cause determines the intervention. A phone call, letter, or referral alone is not an intervention.
Families & caregivers

Why every day matters

Regular attendance keeps scholars connected to learning, trusted adults, friends, activities, and support.

Something is getting in the way

Tell the school what makes attendance hard: transportation, health, housing, anxiety, routines, peer concerns. These can be solved together.
